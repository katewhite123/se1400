Photo Tip - Posing and Portraits

Great portraits come down to small adjustments that make a big difference. Instead of facing the camera straight on, try turning your body slightly to create a more natural, flattering angle. Keep your posture relaxed—drop your shoulders, shift your weight, and avoid stiff poses. For photographers, guide your subject with simple prompts rather than rigid instructions to capture genuine expressions. Pay attention to hands and placement to avoid awkward tension. Use light to highlight the eyes and add depth to the face. The best portraits happen when your subject feels comfortable, so keep the energy easy, encouraging, and natural.
